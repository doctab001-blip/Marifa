Chest X-ray model used by the Marifa Diagnosis Engine for its on-device (in-browser) read.

Model: DenseNet-121 "xrv-all-45rot15trans15scale" from TorchXRayVision, as packaged for the browser by
Chester the AI Radiology Assistant (https://github.com/mlmed/chester-xray, https://mlmed.org/tools/xray/).
Authors: Joseph Paul Cohen, Paul Bertin, Vincent Frappier, Mohammad Hashir, Rupert Brooks, Hadrien Bertrand.
Papers: https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.11210 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.02497

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
The authors state the system is NOT FOR MEDICAL USE. Marifa uses it for teaching and demonstration only.
Files are unmodified copies of models/xrv-all-45rot15trans15scale from the repository above.
