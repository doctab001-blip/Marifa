## Bradyarrhythmia and heart block (India)

| Test | Now | Proposed | Check against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) | At PHC level (ICMR STW). | Complete heart block, Mobitz II or pauses over 3 seconds carry a high risk of asystole: treat as unstable even if the patient looks well, give atropine and prepare pacing. Record it first in every patient with bradycardia, including at PHC level (ICMR STW). | ICMR STW, January 2025, section Investigations |
| Ambulatory ECG (Holter) or loop recorder | Intermittent symptoms (ICMR STW). | Pauses that match symptoms confirm a bradyarrhythmia that needs pacing; no pauses despite symptoms may need a longer recording. Use for intermittent symptoms with a normal resting ECG (ICMR STW). | ICMR STW, January 2025, section Investigations |
| Potassium, urea, creatinine, glucose | All patients (ICMR STW). | Potassium above 6 mEq/L: give calcium gluconate and treat hyperkalaemia first, as it can cause the block; raised urea and creatinine point to a renal cause; low glucose needs correction (ICMR STW). | ICMR STW, January 2025, section Investigations |
| Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) | All patients (ICMR STW). | A raised TSH means hypothyroidism, a reversible cause of bradycardia: start levothyroxine and reassess before deciding on a pacemaker (ICMR STW). | ICMR STW, January 2025, section Investigations |
