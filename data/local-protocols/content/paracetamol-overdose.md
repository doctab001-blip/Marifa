# Paracetamol Overdose

SCOPE: Adult (over 18 years) Emergency Department (ED) assessment and acetylcysteine treatment of acute paracetamol overdose, based on the UK summary of product characteristics for acetylcysteine 200 mg/mL injection (Martindale Pharma, updated 10 September 2025). Treatment lines are those in the label. Children, repeated supratherapeutic ingestion and liver transplant selection beyond the criteria listed are outside this protocol. No Saudi national guideline was found.

## Pathophysiology
Paracetamol overdose causes liver injury when its toxic metabolite exhausts glutathione stores. [1]
- Acetylcysteine replaces glutathione and is most effective within 8–10 hours of ingestion, with declining benefit to 24 hours. [1]
- Poor nutrition, chronic alcohol use and enzyme-inducing drugs increase risk. [1]
- Severe poisoning causes hepatic failure, acute kidney injury, lactic acidosis and death. [1]

## Signs & Symptoms
### Symptoms
- Often none in the first 24 hours; nausea, vomiting, right upper quadrant pain later; confusion and bleeding in liver failure. [1]
### Examination signs
- Right upper quadrant tenderness, jaundice, encephalopathy, low urine output. [1]
### Red flags
- Staggered overdose (taken over 1 hour or more) or doubt about timing. [1]
- Raised alanine aminotransferase (ALT), prolonged international normalised ratio (INR), acidosis, high lactate. [1]
- Co-ingestion of other drugs, or intentional self-harm. [1]
- Presentation after 8 hours. [1]
### Precipitants
- Intentional or accidental ingestion; chronic alcohol use, malnutrition or enzyme-inducing drugs increase risk of liver injury. [1]
### Differentials
| Condition | The one feature that separates it |
|---|---|
| Viral or alcoholic hepatitis | Normal paracetamol level; different history |
| Aspirin or other co-ingestant overdose | Salicylate level raised; tinnitus, tachypnoea |
| Ischaemic hepatitis | Shock or heart failure history |
| Acute fatty liver or HELLP syndrome | Pregnancy |

## Investigations
### Diagnostic criteria
| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Treat on the treatment line | Plasma paracetamol on or above the line from 100 mg/L at 4 hours to 15 mg/L at 15 hours [1] section 4.1 |
| Treat regardless of level | Staggered overdose, or any doubt about time of ingestion [1] section 4.1 |
| Late presentation | Treat up to 24 hours; beyond 24 hours if at risk of severe liver damage [1] section 4.1 |

## Management
### Immediate (0–15 minutes)
- Airway, breathing, circulation; intravenous (IV) access; check capillary glucose; record time of ingestion. [1]
- Send paracetamol level (4 hours after ingestion), ALT, INR, creatinine, gas. [1]
- Start acetylcysteine without waiting for the level if presenting after 8 hours, staggered or timing unknown. [1]
### Ongoing (15–60 minutes)
- Monitor potassium, observations and signs of anaphylactoid reaction during the first infusion. [1]
- Repeat ALT, INR and creatinine at the end of the 21-hour regimen. [1]
### Escalation
- Rising INR, acidosis, encephalopathy or kidney injury: critical care and liver unit. [1]
### Disposition
- Admit to complete treatment; medical clearance and mental health assessment for intentional overdose. [1]

### Guideline comparison
| Parameter | Acetylcysteine label (2025) [1] | Standard UK poisons practice (not opened) [2] |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment line | 100 mg/L at 4 hours to 15 mg/L at 15 hours | Same single line |
| Regimen | 150 mg/kg in 200 mL over 1 hour; 50 mg/kg in 500 mL over 4 hours; 100 mg/kg in 1000 mL over 16 hours | Same |
| Weight cap | 110 kg | 110 kg |
| Charcoal | Not mentioned | 50 g within 1 hour of a large ingestion |
### Discharge criteria
- Completed regimen with normal ALT, INR and creatinine, mental health assessment done, safe discharge plan. [1]
### Follow-up
- Liver and renal tests at 48–72 hours if abnormal; mental health follow-up for intentional overdose. [1]
### Patient education
- Do not take more than the recommended paracetamol dose; store medicines safely.
### Lifestyle and diet
- Reduce alcohol; seek help for distress.
### Team referrals
- Poisons service; liver unit; liaison psychiatry. [1]
### Learning objectives
- Apply the treatment line and give the three-bag acetylcysteine regimen safely.
### Key points
- Treat staggered or unknown-time overdose regardless of level. [1]
- Cap weight at 110 kg. [1]
### Pitfalls
- Waiting for the level after 8 hours. [1]
- Missing hypokalaemia. [1]
- Under-diluting in small patients. [1]
